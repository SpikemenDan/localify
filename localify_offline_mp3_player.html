Tidy names & tags

Names like “A - B” are usuallyUsed when the tags don't say which part is the artist.
AI suggestionsA small model that runs in this browser. It sees only names and tags, never the audio. Downloads once when you first use it.
Fill in song details automaticallyNew songs get a title, artist, album and more in your library. Your files aren't changed until you press Apply in Tidy.
Only run when onlineSongs added while offline are marked Unsorted and filled in once you're back online.
Look up songs onlineAsks MusicBrainz and iTunes for missing details, genres and album covers. Only the song's title and artist text are sent (to musicbrainz.org and itunes.apple.com). No file names, folders or audio are sent. Covers come from coverartarchive.org or Apple and are shown in your library only. With an AcoustID key, songs can also be identified by a fingerprint of their sound.
Auto-apply to filesWrites filled-in details straight into the files, only in folders you've already allowed Localify to edit. Undo from Tidy names & tags.
