Open in Audacity: one-time setup

Browsers can't start programs on their own. A small helper in the tools/audacity folder next to this player adds a localify-audacity: link type. When you click "Open in Audacity", the helper checks that the file is an audio file inside your music folder and opens it in Audacity.

  1. Copy localify-audacity.ps1 to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Localify Audacity Helper\ and put your music folder in its $MusicRoots line (the line below shows the exact text).
  2. Double-click install-localify-audacity.reg. It only changes your own user account and needs no admin rights.
  3. Tell the player where the folder is on this PC.
